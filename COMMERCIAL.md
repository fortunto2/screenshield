# Commercial use and priority

ScreenShield is MIT-licensed. Anyone can use it, ship it, fork it, or sell a product built on it. Nothing here
is hard to reproduce: OCR on the device, a few detectors, a delay buffer and a policy-bound agent.

**Epiphan has first priority on productizing ScreenShield** for lecture capture and live video
(Pearl-class encoders, Epiphan Cloud): the author built it during the Cyberdefense Hackathon (San Francisco,
9 October 2026) while working on AI at Epiphan, and that stream shape (SRT / RTMP / HLS from a room encoder)
is the first real target. Concretely:

- Epiphan gets the first look at any commercial edition (edge app, cloud service, support, policy packs,
  per-tenant archive) and the author's time on it, before anyone else.
- Anything Epiphan-specific that is not useful to the community stays out of this repository.
- This repository stays open under MIT. The priority is about the author's effort and the product, not about
  restricting the code.

This is a statement of intent, not a licence term. If you want to build a product on ScreenShield, go ahead; if
you want to work together on one, the order is Epiphan first, then everyone else. Contact: github.com/fortunto2.
